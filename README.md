# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The dashboard converts sales data into meaningful insights using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, and Slicers**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Track revenue, orders, quantity, and customers
- Identify top customers by revenue
- Compare sales and profit across regions
- Analyze product and item category performance
- Monitor orders over time

---

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 📦 Total Quantity
- 🛍️ Total Orders
- 🧾 Average Order Value
- 👥 Total Customers

---

## 📈 Key Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend
Shows changes in sales performance across different months.

### Sales by Region
Compares sales performance across East, West, North, and South regions.

### Profit by Region
Shows the contribution of each region to overall profit.

### Top 5 Customers
Identifies the customers generating the highest revenue.

### Sales by Product & Category
Compares product and category performance to identify high-performing products.

### Orders Over Time
Tracks the number of orders throughout the year.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

- Month
- Region
- Item Category

These slicers allow users to filter the dashboard and analyze specific areas.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas
- Data Visualization

---

## 💡 Project Outcome

The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance and helps identify **sales trends, profitable regions, top customers, and high-performing products** for better business decision-making.
